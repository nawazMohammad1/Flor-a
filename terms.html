Placing an order

Bouquet orders

We carefully prepare every bouquet according to the details confirmed during your order.

  • Please provide accurate recipient, contact and delivery information.
  • Orders are considered confirmed after the required details and payment arrangements are completed.
  • Flower availability may vary depending on season and local supply.
  • We may make small substitutions while preserving the overall style, value and beauty of the arrangement.

Natural flowers can vary in size, shape, colour and bloom stage. This is part of what makes every Floréa arrangement unique.

Getting your flowers to you

Delivery

Floréa focuses on providing thoughtful local flower delivery. Delivery timing depends on the selected date, location, availability and order requirements.

  • Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address.
  • Requested delivery times are treated as preferences unless a specific time has been confirmed.
  • A working recipient contact number helps us complete the delivery smoothly.
  • Weather, traffic and other circumstances outside our reasonable control may affect delivery timing.

If you are unsure whether we deliver to your location, please contact us before placing your order.
